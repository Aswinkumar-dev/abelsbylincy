Founder's Note

Jewellery That Tells
Your Story

Abel's By Lincy was born from a singular passion: creating fine jewellery that doesn't just sit in a velvet safe for special occasions, but is worn, cherished, and passed down as an enduring legacy.

Every stone is individually examined in our Sydney atelier. We partner exclusively with ethical diamond suppliers following the Kimberley Process, ensuring every sparkle represents honesty, elegance, and integrity.

— Lincy Titus, Founder & Creative Director

Milestones

The Abel's By Lincy Journey

2018

Atelier Founded in Sydney

Lincy started Abel's By Lincy from her studio in Surry Hills, Sydney, with a clear vision: heirloom-quality jewellery for modern Australians.

2020

The Lumière Debut Collection

The premiere Lumière constellation collection — six hand-set diamond pieces — sold out within 48 hours of release.

2022

Atelier Expansion to The Rocks

Relocated to a dedicated heritage atelier in Sydney, opening private consultations for custom engagement rings.

2024

10,000 Milestone Heirlooms

Celebrated over 10,000 bespoke pieces crafted and shipped across Australia, receiving Australian ethical design recognition.

2026

Digital Flagship Experience

Launching the unified digital flagship platform to bring the complete boutique atelier experience directly to customers worldwide.
